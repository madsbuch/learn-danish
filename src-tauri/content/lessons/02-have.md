**[[say: at have]]** means *to have*. Its present tense is **[[say: har]]** for every subject.

- [[say: Jeg har en cykel.]] — I have a bicycle.
- [[say: Du har et hus.]] — You have a house.
- [[say: Hun har to børn.]] — She has two children.
- [[say: Vi har tid.]] — We have time.

Use **en** with common-gender nouns and **et** with neuter nouns. Learn the article with each noun.

### Ask and answer
- [[say: Har du en hund?]] — Do you have a dog?
- [[say: Ja, det har jeg.]] — Yes, I do.
- [[say: Nej, det har jeg ikke.]] — No, I do not.
- [[say: Jeg har ikke en bil.]] — I do not have a car.

### Useful expressions
- [[say: Jeg har brug for hjælp.]] — I need help.
- [[say: Jeg har lyst til en kop te.]] — I feel like a cup of tea.
- [[say: Jeg har det godt.]] — I am doing well.

For age, Danish uses **er**, not *har*: [[say: Jeg er tredive år.]] — I am thirty years old.
